Executive Summary

Across urban and semi-urban Bangladesh, millions of residential flats are managed by individual landlords owning 2 to 20 units. Despite widespread smartphone and mobile financial service (bKash/Nagad) adoption, daily rent operations remain trapped in paper khatas, phone calls, and screenshot galleries. The moment a tenant pays partial rent, splits cash and bKash installments, or disputes a prior month's balance, manual tracking and simple "Paid/Unpaid" apps break down.

RentReady AI solves this with a mobile-first Bangla/English platform that pairs conversational AI speed with deterministic accounting integrity and live Banglalink telco messaging:

Three Core Engineering Pillars

  1. Safe Bangla & Banglish Voice-to-Confirmed-Ledger: A landlord taps the mobile center mic and speaks naturally—"Flat 3B er Rahim bhai 8000 taka bKash korse". Our unified Google Gemini API pipeline (gemini-3.5-flash-lite with automatic failover to gemini-2.5-flash and gemini-2.5-flash-lite) transcribes Bangla audio in-memory (zero audio stored) into an editable transcript, extracts strict Zod-validated JSON, resolves the exact tenant/unit deterministically (never guessing on ambiguous names), and presents a human-readable Bangla/English Confirmation Card before any financial write or SMS executes.
  2. Deterministic, Immutable Financial Ledger: Every taka is stored in PostgreSQL BIGINT *_paisa columns (1 BDT = 100 paisa, zero floating-point math). Balances are never stored as fragile "Paid/Unpaid" booleans; they are computed live inside security_invoker SQL views from rent_obligations minus SUM(payment_allocations). Confirmed payments are append-only and never deleted—corrections create atomic negative reversal rows with full audit trails.
  3. Production-Grade Banglalink AppLink SMS: Confirmed payment receipts and overdue reminders dispatch via POST https://api.applink.com.bd/sms/send through our HMAC-SHA256 authenticated DigitalOcean Basic Static-IPv4 Relay ($4/mo), permanently solving Cloudflare Anycast's E1303 IP-whitelisting constraint while keeping total hackathon infrastructure at $4/month (~500 BDT).

AppQuest 2.0 • Problem Statement Page 3 of 8

Problem Statement

Small residential landlords in Bangladesh (2–20 units) run informal micro-businesses without accountants or dedicated property managers. Field observation across Dhaka landlords reveals four recurring operational breakdowns:

1. Partial-Payment & Balance Drift

Tenants routinely split a ৳12,000 monthly rent into ৳8,000 via bKash on the 5th and ৳4,000 cash on the 15th. Paper khatas and simple "Paid/Unpaid" checkboxes lose track of partial allocations and multi-month arrears.

2. Form Fatigue for Non-Tech Landlords

Many residential building owners (often aged 45–70) abandon English, form-heavy property apps within two weeks and revert to notebooks because logging every entry takes too many taps.

3. Missing Receipts & Awkward Chasing

Cash and partial bKash payments rarely generate instant, timestamped SMS receipts, sparking move-out disputes. Chasing late rent via personal phone calls creates social awkwardness.

4. Zero Net Income Visibility

Water pump repairs, plumbing fixes, and caretaker expenses are paid out of pocket without linking to rent collections—leaving owners blind to true monthly net operating results.

Proposed Solution: Safe Bangla Voice + Verified Ledger

RentReady AI pairs a mobile-first bilingual (বাংলা / English) SPA—featuring a prominent Hero Voice Launcher and a raised bottom-bar Mic button—with a deterministic human-in-the-loop AI pipeline over a single PostgreSQL financial ledger.

The 9-Step Human-in-the-Loop Bangla Voice Pipeline

1. Mic Capture (Browser MediaRecorder, 5 MB cap) OR Direct Bangla/Banglish Text Fallback 2. In-Memory Bangla ASR (Google Gemini Native Multimodal Audio — zero audio files stored) 3. Editable Transcript UI (Landlord reviews or corrects any misheard name or Taka amount) 4. Structured JSON Extraction (Gemini API: temperature=0, JSON MIME, 3-model 429 failover) 5. Strict Zod Validation (Rejects negative amounts, missing fields, or malformed JSON) 6. Deterministic DB Entity Resolution (Normalizes "Flat/ফ্ল্যাট/bhai/ভাই"; NEVER guesses) └─ If 2 tenants named "Rahim" exist → returns `needs_clarification` radio picker 7. Human-Readable Bangla/English Confirmation Card (Shows Tenant, Unit, BDT, Remaining Due / SMS) 8. Deterministic Backend Execution (Idempotent PostgreSQL RPC or Confirmed AppLink SMS) 9. Append-Only Audit Trail (`voice_sessions` → `voice_actions` → `audit_logs` / `notification_logs`)

8 Structured Voice Action Contracts (+ Safe unknown Fallback)

Write & SMS Actions (Confirmation Mandatory)

  • record_rent_payment — Full/partial payment (cash/bKash/Nagad/bank)
  • record_expense — Property repair, utility, or caretaker expense
  • create_maintenance_request — Log unit issue with priority level
  • prepare_rent_reminder — Draft & confirm AppLink SMS reminder

Read-Only Queries (Human-Readable Answer Card)

  • get_outstanding_rent — Exact due list per unit, tenant & month
  • get_monthly_collection — Expected vs. collected vs. net income
  • get_payment_history — Chronological tenant ledger & reversals
  • get_property_summary — Occupancy, expenses & open maintenance

Complete Core Operations (Touch UI + Voice Parity)

  • Properties & Units: Streamlined setup (auto-creates default building), default rent in exact paisa, and live occupied/vacant status tracking.
  • Tenants & Tenancies: Normalized Bangladesh numbers (+8801XXXXXXXXX), Banglalink prefix badge, and DB-enforced single active tenancy per unit (uq_tenancies_one_active_per_unit).
  • Immutable Financial Ledger: 1-click idempotent monthly bill generation (Asia/Dhaka), FIFO partial/full payment allocation, immutable reversals with reason, and 1-click confirmed AppLink SMS receipts.
  • Warm Customer UX: Human-readable Bangla/English voice cards (VoiceConfirmationCard & VoiceAnswerCard) and friendly localized error translation (toFriendlyError)—zero raw JSON or technical stack traces shown to landlords.

Technical Implementation & Security Architecture

RentReady AI is engineered as a strictly typed TypeScript modular monolith (pnpm workspace) combining code-split React 19 static assets with a Hono edge API on Cloudflare Workers—built for sub-second mobile loads and zero-rewrite production scaling.

[Mobile-First SPA: React 19 + Vite 6 (Code-Split) + Tailwind v4 (বাংলা / English)] │ HTTPS + Supabase User JWT (Single Origin via Cloudflare Workers Static Assets) ▼ [Cloudflare Worker: Hono REST API (apps/api) + Security Headers] ├──► [Supabase PostgreSQL + Auth + 100% RLS (19 Tables, BIGINT *_paisa, Atomic RPCs)] ├──► [Google Gemini API (gemini-3.5-flash-lite → 2.5-flash → 2.5-flash-lite failover)] │ └─ Native In-Memory Bangla Audio ASR + Strict Zod-Validated JSON Extraction └──► Web Crypto HMAC-SHA256 + 60s Replay Guard ──► [DigitalOcean $4/mo Static-IPv4 Relay] │ (Whitelisted Static IPv4) ▼ [Banglalink AppLink SMS API]

Financial Ledger Invariants

  • Zero Floats: All money stored in BIGINT *_paisa (1 BDT = 100 paisa) with exact BigInt string parsing in @rentready/shared.
  • Derived Balances: v_obligation_balances & v_dashboard_summary compute balances & KPIs directly in SQL (Asia/Dhaka timezone).
  • Idempotent Atomic RPCs: record_payment_atomic & reverse_payment_atomic enforce UNIQUE(landlord_id, idempotency_key).

Security & Multi-Tenant Isolation

  • 100% RLS Coverage: All 19/19 public tables enforce landlord_id = auth.uid(); views use security_invoker = true and RPCs run as SECURITY INVOKER.
  • Zero service_role in User Routes: Worker validates the caller's JWT and forwards it directly to PostgreSQL.
  • Append-Only & Privacy: Zero UPDATE/DELETE policies on payments, payment_allocations, notification_logs, and audit_logs. No NID photos or audio files stored.

Automated Quality Verification (GitHub Actions CI)

Every push to main runs strict TypeScript typechecking, Vite 6 production code-split builds, and 35/35 automated Vitest tests verifying integer money math, SQL migration RLS/no-float invariants, ambiguous voice entity rejection, Gemini HTTP 429 model failover, HMAC replay protection, and HTTP 200 AppLink error parsing (E1303/E1313).

Banglalink AppLink Integration: APIs & User Flows

Banglalink AppLink is wired directly into RentReady AI's core financial loop—turning every confirmed rent payment and overdue balance into an instant, auditable telco communication channel.

1. APIs Integrated & Production Roadmap

AppLink API Endpoint RentReady AI Use Case Stage
SMS MT API POST https://api.applink.com.bd/sms/send Confirmed Bangla/English payment receipts (showing paid amount & remaining due) and polite overdue rent reminders Live in MVP (Release A)
OTP API POST /otp/request & /otp/verify Banglalink landlord phone verification & high-value payment reversal confirmation Enhancement Sprint (6–16 Dec)

2. Solving the Cloudflare-to-AppLink Static-IP Blocker (E1303)

Official AppLink authentication requires every API POST to originate from an explicitly whitelisted static IPv4 address (unprovisioned IPs return E1303). Because Cloudflare Workers use rotating Anycast egress IPs, direct Worker-to-AppLink calls fail in production. We engineered an ultra-low-cost ($4/mo), hardened relay:

  • apps/api signs each confirmed SMS payload using Web Crypto HMAC-SHA256 over {timestampSec}.{rawBody}.
  • services/applink-relay (Dockerized Node 22 / Hono container on a DigitalOcean Basic Static-IPv4 Droplet — $4/mo, SGP1) verifies the signature with crypto.timingSafeEqual and a strict <= 60s replay window, enforces a hardcoded allowlist strictly to https://api.applink.com.bd/sms/send (preventing SSRF), injects server-side applicationId + password, and dispatches from our single whitelisted static IPv4.

3. Telco-Grade Error Handling & User Flow (Per Applink-as-a-Skill)

Landlord records ৳8,000 payment (Unit 3B) OR triggers Voice/Dashboard "SMS Reminder" └─► Confirmation Modal / Voice Card opens (shows +88019..., editable Bangla/English SMS) └─► Landlord clicks "Confirm & Execute" (sends confirmed:true + idempotency_key) └─► Worker checks duplicate key ─► Signs & calls DigitalOcean Static-IPv4 Relay └─► AppLink POST /sms/send ─► Parses JSON body (NEVER trusts HTTP 200 alone!) ├─ statusCode === "S1000" ─► status="sent", logs requestId └─ E1303 / E1313 / other ─► status="failed", logs sanitized code

Operator Transparency & Test-Data Compliance: Because AppLink natively serves Banglalink subscribers (+88019 / +88014), RentReady AI detects recipient prefixes, displays a clear compatibility indicator, and offers a 1-click "Copy Text" fallback for non-Banglalink numbers until our multi-operator BD SMS gateway launches in Release C. All build-phase demos use synthetic test data only (+8801900000001 / +8801900000002 per Booklet p. 17).
