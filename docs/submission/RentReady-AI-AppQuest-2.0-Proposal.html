Across urban and semi-urban Bangladesh, millions of residential flats are managed by individual landlords owning 2 to 20 units. Three Core Engineering Pillars

  1. Safe Bangla & Banglish Voice-to-Confirmed-Ledger: A landlord taps the Hero Voice Launcher or raised bottom-bar mic and speaks naturally—"ফ্ল্যাট 3B এর রহিম ভাই ৮০০০ টাকা বিকাশ করেছে". Our unified Google Gemini API pipeline (gemini-3.5-flash-lite with automatic HTTP 429/5xx failover to gemini-2.5-flash and gemini-2.5-flash-lite) transcribes audio in-memory (zero audio stored) into an editable transcript, extracts strict Zod-validated JSON, resolves the exact tenant/unit deterministically (never guessing on ambiguous matches), and gates every write or SMS behind a human-readable Bangla/English Confirmation Card.
  2. Deterministic, Immutable Financial Ledger: Every taka is stored in PostgreSQL BIGINT *_paisa columns (1 BDT = 100 paisa, zero floating-point math). Balances are derived live inside security_invoker SQL views from rent_obligations minus SUM(payment_allocations). Confirmed payments are append-only and never hard-deleted—corrections create atomic negative reversal rows with full audit trails.
  3. Production-Grade Banglalink AppLink SMS: Confirmed payment receipts and overdue reminders dispatch via POST https://api.applink.com.bd/sms/send through our HMAC-SHA256 authenticated DigitalOcean Basic Static-IPv4 Relay ($4/mo), permanently solving Cloudflare Anycast's E1303 IP-whitelisting blocker while keeping total hackathon infrastructure at $4/month (~500 BDT).

AppQuest 2.0 • Problem Statement Page 3 of 8

Problem Statement

Small residential landlords in Bangladesh (2–20 units) operate informal micro-businesses without accountants or property managers. Market research and landlord interviews highlight four structural pain points that cause recurring revenue leakage and tenant disputes:

1. Partial-Payment & Balance Drift

Tenants frequently split a ৳12,000 monthly rent into ৳8,000 via bKash on the 5th and ৳4,000 cash on the 15th. Paper khatas and simple "Paid/Unpaid" toggles lose exact remaining balances across multi-installment and multi-month arrears.

2. Form Fatigue for Non-Tech Landlords

Many residential building owners (aged 45–70) abandon English, form-heavy property apps within two weeks and revert to paper notebooks because entering every transaction takes too many screens.

3. Missing Receipts & Awkward Chasing

Cash and partial bKash payments rarely produce instant, timestamped SMS receipts, causing disputes at move-out. Chasing late rent by personal phone call creates social friction.

4. Zero Expense vs. Income Visibility

Water pump repairs, plumbing fixes, and caretaker costs are paid out of pocket without linking to monthly rent collections—leaving owners blind to true net operating income.

Proposed Solution: Safe Bangla Voice + Verified Ledger

RentReady AI delivers a Bangladeshi mobile-first bilingual (বাংলা / English) operations app inspired by familiar TallyKhata/bKash ergonomics—featuring a prominent Hero Voice Launcher (30s auto-stop + live waveform), a raised mobile bottom-bar Mic button, quick-action tiles, and slide-up mobile bottom sheets over one deterministic PostgreSQL ledger.

The 9-Step Human-in-the-Loop Bangla Voice Pipeline

1. Mic Capture (Browser MediaRecorder, 30s timer, 5 MB cap) OR Direct Bangla/Banglish Text 2. In-Memory Bangla ASR (Google Gemini Native Multimodal Audio — zero audio files stored) 3. Editable Transcript UI (Landlord reviews or corrects any misheard name or Taka amount) 4. Structured JSON Extraction (Gemini API: temperature=0, JSON MIME, 3-model 429 failover) 5. Strict Zod Validation (Rejects negative amounts, missing fields, or malformed JSON) 6. Deterministic DB Entity Resolution (Normalizes "Flat/ফ্ল্যাট/bhai/ভাই"; NEVER guesses) └─ If 2 tenants named "Rahim" exist → returns `needs_clarification` radio-button picker 7. Human-Readable Confirmation Card (Shows Tenant, Unit, BDT, Remaining Due / Editable SMS) 8. Deterministic Backend Execution (Idempotent PostgreSQL RPC or Confirmed AppLink SMS) 9. Append-Only Audit Trail (`voice_sessions` → `voice_actions` → `audit_logs` / `notification_logs`)

8 Structured Voice Action Contracts (+ Safe unknown Fallback)

Write & SMS Actions (Confirmation Mandatory)

  • record_rent_payment — Full/partial payment (cash/bKash/Nagad/bank)
  • record_expense — Property repair, utility, or caretaker expense
  • create_maintenance_request — Log unit issue with priority level
  • prepare_rent_reminder — Draft & confirm AppLink SMS reminder

Read-Only Queries (Human-Readable Answer Card)

  • get_outstanding_rent — Exact due list per unit, tenant & month
  • get_monthly_collection — Expected vs. collected vs. net result
  • get_payment_history — Chronological tenant ledger & reversals
  • get_property_summary — Occupancy, expenses & open repairs

Production-Grade Operations (Touch UI + Voice Parity)

  • TallyKhata-Style Dashboard: Warm Bangla greeting, Asia/Dhaka month badge, 4-button Quick Actions strip, animated Monthly Collection Progress Bar, 6 DB-computed KPI cards, and overdue tenant cards with 1-tap Call & 1-tap SMS Reminder.

Technical Implementation & Security Architecture

RentReady AI is built as a strictly typed TypeScript modular monolith (pnpm workspace) pairing a code-split React 19 / Vite 6 SPA with a Hono edge API on Cloudflare Workers Static Assets—engineered for sub-second mobile performance and commercial scaling without a rewrite.

Financial Ledger Invariants

  • Zero Floats: All money stored in BIGINT *_paisa (1 BDT = 100 paisa) with exact BigInt parsing in @rentready/shared.
  • Derived Balances: v_obligation_balances & v_dashboard_summary compute balances & KPIs directly in SQL (Asia/Dhaka).
  • Idempotent Atomic RPCs: record_payment_atomic & reverse_payment_atomic enforce UNIQUE(landlord_id, idempotency_key).

Security & Multi-Tenant Isolation

  • 100% RLS Coverage: All 19/19 tables enforce landlord_id = auth.uid(); views use security_invoker = true and RPCs run as SECURITY INVOKER.
  • Zero service_role in User Routes: Worker validates and forwards the caller's Supabase JWT directly to PostgreSQL.
  • Append-Only & Privacy: Zero UPDATE/DELETE policies on payments, payment_allocations, notification_logs, and audit_logs. No NID photos or audio files stored.

Automated Quality Verification (GitHub Actions CI)

Every push to main runs strict TypeScript typechecking, Vite 6 code-split builds, and 36/36 automated Vitest tests verifying integer money math, SQL migration RLS/no-float invariants, ambiguous voice rejection, Gemini HTTP 429 model failover, zero demo/hackathon UI copy, HMAC replay protection, and HTTP 200 AppLink error parsing (E1303/E1313).

Banglalink AppLink Integration: APIs & User Flows

Banglalink AppLink is wired directly into RentReady AI's core financial loop—turning every confirmed rent payment and overdue balance into an instant, auditable telco communication channel.

1. APIs Integrated & Production Roadmap

AppLink API Endpoint RentReady AI Use Case Stage
SMS MT API POST https://api.applink.com.bd/sms/send Confirmed Bangla/English payment receipts (paid amount + remaining due) & polite overdue rent reminders Live in MVP (Release A)
OTP API POST /otp/request & /otp/verify Banglalink landlord phone verification & high-value payment reversal confirmation Enhancement Sprint (6–16 Dec)

2. Solving the Cloudflare-to-AppLink Static-IP Blocker (E1303)

Official AppLink authentication requires every API POST to originate from an explicitly whitelisted static IPv4 address (unprovisioned IPs return E1303). Because Cloudflare Workers operate over rotating Anycast egress IPs, direct Worker-to-AppLink calls fail in production. We engineered an ultra-low-cost ($4/mo), hardened relay:

  • apps/api signs each confirmed SMS payload using Web Crypto HMAC-SHA256 over {timestampSec}.{rawBody}.
  • services/applink-relay (Dockerized Node 22 / Hono container on a DigitalOcean Basic Static-IPv4 Droplet — $4/mo, SGP1) verifies the signature with crypto.timingSafeEqual and a strict <= 60s replay guard, enforces a hardcoded allowlist strictly to https://api.applink.com.bd/sms/send (preventing SSRF), injects server-side applicationId + password (never logged or returned), and dispatches from our single whitelisted static IPv4.

3. Telco-Grade Error Handling & User Flow (Per Applink-as-a-Skill)

Landlord records ৳8,000 payment (Unit 3B) OR triggers Voice/Dashboard "SMS Reminder" └─► Mobile Bottom-Sheet Modal opens (shows +88019..., editable Bangla/English SMS) └─► Landlord taps "Send SMS" (dispatches confirmed:true + unique idempotency_key) └─► Worker checks duplicate key ─► Signs & calls DigitalOcean Static-IPv4 Relay └─► AppLink POST /sms/send ─► Parses JSON body (NEVER trusts HTTP 200 alone!) ├─ statusCode === "S1000" ─► status="sent", logs requestId └─ E1303 / E1313 / other ─► status="failed", logs sanitized code

Operator Honesty & Instant Practical Fallbacks: AppLink natively serves Banglalink subscribers (+88019 / +88014). RentReady AI automatically detects Banglalink prefixes for direct AppLink SMS, while giving non-Banglalink numbers instant 1-tap WhatsApp (wa.me) and Copy Text fallbacks until our multi-operator BD SMS aggregator launches in Release C.

Test-Data Compliance (Booklet p. 17): All build-phase demos use synthetic Banglalink test numbers (+8801900000001 / +8801900000002) via pnpm seed:demo; live SMS is never fired inside automated CI.
